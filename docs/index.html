Max Watch

A 100% offline, free, encrypted Mac & Windows motion & sound security camera.

Turn your MacBook, iMac, Windows laptop, desktop PC, or external USB webcam into a motion-activated (and optional sound-wake) security recorder that keeps recording while your screen is locked—illuminating dark rooms automatically at night. Footage encrypts in memory into a password-protected AES-256 vault with zero cloud accounts, zero subscriptions, and zero network access.

Motion + Additive “Also Wake on Sound?”

Dial motion sensitivity (10%–100%) and optionally add sound-wake triggers (never replacing motion) via an interactive drag slider with pop-up checkpoints like “Normal Talking Voice” and 1.25s RAM pre-roll.

Night Visibility When Screen Is Locked

Arm and press Ctrl+Cmd+Q on Mac or Win+L on Windows. Max Brightness When Locked (checked by default, uncheckable anytime) keeps the lock screen at 100% brightness so cameras see clearly in pitch-dark rooms, then restores your brightness on unlock.

Strictly Locked AES-256 Vault

Clips encrypt in RAM via Curve25519 + AES-256-GCM while the vault stays locked during recording, so nobody entering a room can press Play All or delete clips without your password.

Power-Cut & Exit Safe

1-second encrypted fMP4 segments sync to disk every second so recordings survive sudden power loss, and quitting flushes active clips cleanly before exit.

Fast Scrubber Ball & 1x / 4x / 12x Speeds

Drag the timeline scrubber ball to skim footage rapidly in RAM with zero disk temp files, or review recordings sequentially at Normal (1x), 4x, or 12x speed.

One-Click Save & Next + Delete & Next

Browsing many motion clips is effortless: export standard MP4 copies to any chosen folder on your Mac or Windows PC with Save & Next, or zero-wipe unwanted clips with Delete & Next.
