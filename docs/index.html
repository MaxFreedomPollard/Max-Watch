Max Watch

A free, 100% offline motion-detection security camera and AES-256 encrypted video vault for macOS, built and gifted to the community by Max Freedom Pollard. Works on every Apple Silicon and Intel Mac (macOS 13.0+), even while your screen is locked.

Works While Screen Is Locked

Arm Max Watch and press Ctrl+Cmd+Q to lock your Mac. Motion detection and encrypted recording continue uninterrupted in the background.

Strictly Locked While Saving

Every clip is encrypted in memory while the vault stays locked during recording, so nobody entering a room can press Play All or delete clips without your password.

Power-Cut & Exit Safe

1-second encrypted fMP4 segments sync to disk continuously so clips survive sudden power loss, and quitting flushes active recordings cleanly.

Motion Detect Sliding Scale

Dial in subtle movement or heavy-motion-only triggers with a live motion energy meter and 48 kHz AAC microphone meter.

Fast-Drag Ball, Save & Next, Delete & Next

Slide the luminous second ball for instant scrubbing at 1x, 4x, or 12x speed, and triage clips fast with one-click Save & Next (to any Mac folder) or Delete & Next.

Kernel-Enforced Zero Network

Sandboxed by macOS with zero network entitlements. No cloud accounts, no subscriptions, no telemetry, and no network sockets ever.
